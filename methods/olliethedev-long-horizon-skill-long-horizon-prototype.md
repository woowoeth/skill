# Long Horizon prototype

This is a disposable candidate for behavioral evaluation, not an installed production skill.

Read the current responsibility, its authorization and status, and the latest signals. Reconstruct active objectives and unfinished actions from the workspace before deciding what to do. An ongoing responsibility continues across individual improvements while useful work remains; a bounded assignment may end when its brief is satisfied or impossible within its constraints. A task paused for owner intervention stays paused until explicitly resumed.

Use the current brief as a starting point, then retrieve relevant earlier actions and supporting evidence. Search by the problem, affected product area, intervention, and related names; use available indexes to locate records. Recent summaries are navigation aids. Follow consequential historical claims to their evidence, including later corrections and related responsibilities' findings. Check the subject, version, cohort, timing, and measurement definition before applying an old conclusion to today's problem.

Distinguish recommendations, implementation, verified deployment, measured outcomes, and rollback. A missing receipt or unavailable observation does not establish failure. Reconcile an uncertain external action before repeating it. Preserve the original record when a conclusion is corrected, linking the correction and its evidence.

Choose a useful objective and next action within the owner's brief. Explain how the evidence supports that choice, what remains uncertain, and what observation would justify the next decision. A past failure can justify a different approach; changed conditions can justify reconsidering it. Shared history can inform the decision without transferring another responsibility's authority or claiming its work as your own.

Before product changes, inspect related ongoing work and actual deployed state. Coordinate according to impact so compatible experiments, fixes, and features can coexist. Record changes that alter the interpretation of an evaluation. Use Impulse for future execution where the assignment authorizes it; a local intended time is not a confirmed schedule.

Leave a concise current handoff plus durable action and evidence references. Retain action records and decision evidence for the responsibility's lifetime. Record what happened, why it was chosen, its outcome or unresolved state, and what the next run should inspect. Respect the current assignment's permitted tools and outputs.
