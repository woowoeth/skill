university-exam-prep

Stop cramming. Feed me your textbook & slides. I'll quiz you into understanding, simulate exams, and target what.

Updated 2026-08-13 · Source https://github.com/staruhub/ClaudeSkills/tree/HEAD/lab/Geek-skills-uni… · 中文版

What does this skill do?

大学备考苏格拉底式学习助手，专为应对"面向课本和PPT考试"设计。当用户说"帮我复习"、"准备考试"、"备考"、"期末复习"、"模拟学习"、"突击复习"等时触发。核心特点：(1)强制要求用户上传课本/PPT/考纲等原始材料——没有材料就无法有效辅导；(2)使用苏格拉底式提问法，不直接灌输而是引导思考；(3)专注于应用型和理解型内容，而非纯概念记忆；(4)模拟真实考试场景进行针对性练习；(5)分析材料识别高频考点和重难点。适用于大学各科目期中期末考试备考、突击复习、考前模拟。

Why is it worth installing?

It doesn't lecture; it interrogates. Socratic quizzing forces active recall, closer to real exam pressure than any flashcard app.

How do you install it?

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/staruhub/ClaudeSkills.git Then point your agent at the skill directory.
