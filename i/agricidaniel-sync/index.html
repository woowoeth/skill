What does this skill do?

Maintain the Sync brain: ingest sources, synthesize canon, render reports, lint the vault, and audit maturity. Use when the user wants to add evidence to Sync, refresh its research, check its audit score, or edit its doctrine. For running actual work through the council (routing, briefs, Codex dispatch, verification gates, measurement), use the `sync` skill instead, not this one.

Why is it worth installing?

Unlike mere storage tools, it audits your vault's maturity and flags gaps—a health check for your knowledge brain.

How do you install it?

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/sync.git Then point your agent at the skill directory.
