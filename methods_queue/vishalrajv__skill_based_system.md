# Story Arc Architect Skill (1-Minute Reel Pacing)

## Purpose
Transforms stories into high-retention episodic arcs optimized for **60-second (1-minute)** short-form vertical video (Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts) using **6 distinct 10-second shots with Start and End frame keyframe pairs** and **pure character acting dialogue**.

---

## 1-Minute Reel Pacing Mathematics (6 Shots x 10.0s Each)

| Element | Target Range | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Duration per Reel** | Exactly **60.0 Seconds (1.0 Minute)** | Maximum algorithmic monetization & retention |
| **Shot Count** | 6 Distinct Shots (10.0s each) | Balanced cinematic pacing with clear scene transitions |
| **Spoken Dialogue Count** | 120 – 150 Spoken Words (~20-25 words/shot) | Natural dramatic cadence with pauses and SFX |
| **Shot 01 (00s – 10s)** | Dramatic Hook / Confrontation | Immediate scroll-stopping conflict & high-stakes dialogue |
| **Shot 02 (10s – 20s)** | Escalation & Environmental Discovery | Clue reveal / forensic inspection / tactical movement |
| **Shot 03 (20s – 30s)** | Rising Conflict & Counter-Dialogue | Verbal duel / escalating tension / alarm trigger |
| **Shot 04 (30s – 40s)** | Action Standoff / Physical Trigger | Gunpoint standoff / desperate attempt / rapid pursuit |
| **Shot 05 (40s – 50s)** | Climax Unmasking & Shock Truth | Major plot twist revelation / high-voltage conflict |
| **Shot 06 (50s – 60s)** | Cliffhanger Ending & Cutoff | Unresolved secret, deadly countdown, snap blackout |

---
